## Ingredients

- 8 chicken thighs, bone-in and skin-on (about 3 lb)
- 2 tbsp olive oil [pantry]
- 2 tsp kosher salt [pantry]
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp black pepper [pantry]
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- 4 sprigs fresh thyme [optional]

## Steps

1. **Heat the oven to 425°F.** Set a rack in the upper middle. {15m, passive, oven 425F}
2. **Dry and season the chicken.** Pat the thighs very dry with paper towels, then rub with the oil, salt, paprika, garlic powder and pepper. {10m, active, with 1}
3. **Roast skin side up.** Arrange on a sheet pan with the lemon wedges and thyme. Roast until the skin is deep golden and the thickest thigh reads 175°F. {35-45m, passive, oven 425F}
4. **Rest.** Let the chicken rest on the pan, then squeeze the roasted lemon over it. {5m, passive}

## Notes

- **Drier skin, crisper skin.** If you have time, salt the thighs and leave them uncovered in the fridge for a few hours first.
- Thighs stay juicy well past 165°F. Taking them to 175°F renders the fat under the skin.
