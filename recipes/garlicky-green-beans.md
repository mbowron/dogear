## Ingredients

- 1 lb green beans, trimmed
- 1/4 cup water [pantry]
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt [pantry]
- 2 tbsp butter
- 3 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes [optional]
- 1 tsp lemon juice

## Steps

1. **Trim the beans and slice the garlic.** {8m, active}
2. **Steam the beans.** Put the beans, water and salt in a large skillet over medium-high heat, cover, and cook until bright green and just tender. {5m, active, burner}
3. **Add the butter and garlic.** Uncover and let the water cook off, then add the butter, garlic and pepper flakes. Toss until the garlic is golden. {3-4m, active, burner}
4. **Finish with lemon.** Toss with the lemon juice and taste for salt. {1m, active}
