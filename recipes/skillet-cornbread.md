## Ingredients

### Dry

- 1 cup cornmeal (150 g)
- 1 cup all-purpose flour (125 g)
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1 tsp kosher salt [pantry]

### Wet

- 1 1/4 cups buttermilk
- 2 large eggs
- 5 tbsp butter, melted (4 for the batter, 1 for the skillet)

## Steps

1. **Heat the oven to 400°F with the skillet inside.** Put a 10-inch cast-iron skillet on the middle rack while the oven heats. {20m, passive, oven 400F}
2. **Mix the dry ingredients.** Whisk the cornmeal, flour, sugar, baking powder and salt in a large bowl. {5m, active, with 1}
3. **Mix the wet ingredients and combine.** Whisk the buttermilk and eggs, then 4 tablespoons of the melted butter. Stir into the dry ingredients until just combined. {5m, active, after 2}
4. **Butter the hot skillet and pour in the batter.** Swirl the last tablespoon of butter in the skillet, pour in the batter and smooth the top. {2m, active}
5. **Bake.** Bake until golden and a toothpick in the center comes out clean. {20-25m, passive, oven 400F}
6. **Cool briefly.** Cool 10 minutes in the skillet before cutting into wedges. {10m, passive}
