## Ingredients

- 1 cup old-fashioned oats (80 g)
- 1 cup milk
- 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp chia seeds
- 2 tbsp maple syrup
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- a pinch of kosher salt [pantry]
- 1 cup berries, for serving

## Steps

1. **Stir everything together.** Mix everything but the berries and split between two jars. {5m, active}
2. **Chill overnight.** At least 8 hours and up to 3 days. {8h, passive, fridge}
3. **Top and eat.** Stir, loosen with a splash of milk if it is thick, and top with the berries. {2m, active}
